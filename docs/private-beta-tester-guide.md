# Spider Private Beta Tester Guide

Spider is a macOS menu bar companion that helps you start building a Meta Ads
campaign from your screen.

Spider guides. You click.

Spider will not:

- publish ads;
- change budget;
- edit billing;
- pause or delete campaigns;
- guarantee approval;
- guarantee sales.

Use a Meta Ads account where you are comfortable testing campaign setup. Do not
test with a client account or high-risk budget unless the founder is watching
the session with you.

## Install

1. Open the Spider DMG.
2. Drag Spider to Applications.
3. Open Spider from Applications.
4. If macOS asks whether you want to open the app, confirm only if the app is
   signed and notarized by the expected developer.

## Sign In

1. Click Spider in the menu bar.
2. Enter your email.
3. Open the magic-link email.
4. Click the login link.
5. Return to Spider after the app opens.

If the login link does not open Spider, tell the founder which browser and email
client you used.

## Grant Permissions

Spider needs macOS permissions to understand the screen and guide the next step.

Grant these when asked:

- Screen Recording
- Accessibility
- Microphone

After granting Screen Recording or Accessibility, macOS may require restarting
Spider. That is normal.

Spider should not ask for:

- your Meta password;
- two-factor authentication codes;
- API keys;
- bank data;
- tax data;
- card numbers.

## Start A Campaign

1. Open Meta Ads Manager in your browser.
2. Open Spider from the menu bar.
3. Click `Build my campaign from scratch`.
4. Fill in:
   - offer;
   - audience;
   - ticket or price;
   - country;
   - language;
   - budget;
   - business goal;
   - landing page;
   - experience level.
5. Click `Build campaign direction`.
6. Read the recommended objective and what Spider says not to choose.
7. Click `Open Meta Ads and guide me`.

The first useful moment should be campaign direction from your actual offer. If
Spider starts by acting like a generic chatbot, report that.

## Guided Setup

Follow Spider one step at a time.

During setup, Spider may:

- explain the visible Meta screen;
- recommend an objective;
- warn about Traffic vs Sales or Leads;
- point to a visible safe control;
- tell you to stop before Publish;
- ask for manual confirmation.

Spider should not:

- click for you;
- publish;
- increase budget;
- edit billing;
- pause or delete anything.

## Locked Features

Preflight and 72h Review are not available in this beta. If you see them as
working actions, report it. Publishing is always your manual decision outside
Spider.

## What To Report

Send feedback on:

- where you got stuck;
- whether Spider understood the Meta screen;
- whether the campaign objective made sense;
- whether Spider warned before risky actions;
- whether any text felt confusing;
- whether Spider ever seemed ready to spend, publish, or change billing.

If anything looks unsafe, stop and tell the founder before continuing.
